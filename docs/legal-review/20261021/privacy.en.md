# MALMOIM Privacy Policy

MALMOIM (hereinafter “MALMOIM”) is a nonprofit language exchange community jointly operated by its organizers. To protect users’ personal information and address related complaints, MALMOIM complies with applicable laws, including the Personal Information Protection Act, and establishes this Privacy Policy as follows.

## Article 1 (Purposes of Processing Personal Information)

MALMOIM processes personal information to the extent necessary for the following purposes.

1. **Registration and account management:** Registration, checking eligibility to register at age 19 or older, login and member identification, account security, and membership withdrawal
2. **Providing language exchange services:** Understanding members’ language proficiency, operating language exchange activities, and providing community services
3. **Operating the board:** Displaying authors of posts and comments, managing content, receiving reports, and enforcing community operating policies
4. **Operating studies and events:** Receiving applications, identifying participants, providing schedule information, checking participation fees, managing participation, and processing refunds
5. **Handling inquiries and complaints:** Receiving and responding to inquiries, resolving disputes, and communicating as necessary to operate the service
6. **Service security:** Preventing misuse, managing access rights, and maintaining system stability

If MALMOIM needs to use personal information for purposes other than those above, it follows the procedures required by applicable law.

## Article 2 (Personal Information Processed and Legal Bases)

### ① Registration

MALMOIM processes the following information during registration.

**Required information**

| Personal information | Purpose of processing |
|---|---|
| Email address | Member identification, login, and account management |
| Password | Member authentication and account protection |
| Nickname | Member identification within the community and author attribution |
| Date of birth | Checking eligibility to register at age 19 or older |
| Korean proficiency | Providing Korean–English language exchange services |
| English proficiency | Providing Korean–English language exchange services |

**Optional information**

| Personal information | Purpose of processing |
|---|---|
| Name | Service purposes notified separately |
| Phone number and country calling code | Contact and notification purposes notified separately |
| Nationality | Language and cultural exchange purposes notified separately |
| Gender | Service purposes notified separately |
| Additional languages and proficiency | Providing additional language exchange information |

Basic registration and community services remain available without providing optional information.

Personal information actually necessary for registration and service provision is processed on applicable legal bases, including Article 15(1)(4) of the Personal Information Protection Act. Where processing is based on separate consent, MALMOIM provides notice of the collection purpose, information collected, retention period, and right to refuse consent, and obtains consent.

The date of birth entered during registration is used to check eligibility, and the operating principle is to retain it until membership withdrawal. However, the need to continue retaining the full date of birth is reviewed in accordance with the principle of data minimization.

Passwords are protected through the authentication system, and MALMOIM does not display passwords on the board or in public profiles.

### ② Board and community activities

- Posts and comments: Author nickname, content, and creation and modification times
- Reports and moderation actions: Report details, reported subject, processing outcome, and necessary member identifiers

The author nicknames of posts and comments are disclosed to other users.

### ③ Study and event applications

Depending on the event, the following information may be processed to the extent necessary.

- Information necessary to identify participants, such as the applicant’s name or nickname and contact information
- The event applied for, application details, and participation status
- For advance bank transfers: Depositor name, deposit amount, deposit date and time, and payment status
- For on-site payments: Amount paid and payment status
- Bank name, account number, account holder name, refund amount, and processing status needed for refunds

The specific information collected and whether it is required or optional are explained at each event application stage.

### ④ Inquiries and service use

MALMOIM may process contact information and inquiry content provided by users when making inquiries.

Authentication cookies, access information, and information generated through service use may also be processed for login and security functions.

## Article 3 (Processing and Retention Periods)

MALMOIM retains personal information according to the following criteria and destroys it after the purpose of processing has been achieved.

| Category | Retention period |
|---|---|
| Member account and profile information | Until membership withdrawal |
| Reports and sanction records | Three years after processing is completed |
| General inquiry records | Six months after processing is completed |
| Event application and participant information | Destroyed without delay after the event ends and necessary settlement is completed |
| Account information collected separately for a refund | Destroyed without delay after the refund is completed |
| Posts and comments | May remain after membership withdrawal, with author-identifying information handled separately |
| Transaction and dispute records that must be retained by law | The period required by the applicable law |

Where personal information must be preserved under applicable law, the necessary information is kept separately for the legally required period and is not used for other purposes.

## Article 4 (Membership Withdrawal and Content)

① Members may request withdrawal through the withdrawal procedure provided by MALMOIM or the privacy contact.

② Upon withdrawal, MALMOIM destroys the member account and personal information unless there is a legitimate reason to retain them, such as a preservation obligation under another law.

③ Posts and comments written by members may remain after withdrawal to preserve the context of community conversations and the convenience of other members.

④ The author label on a withdrawn member’s posts and comments is changed to “탈퇴한 회원(Former Member),” and the public author information is handled so that it is not linked to the withdrawn member’s account.

⑤ Personal information entered directly by a member in a post or comment may not be deleted merely by changing the author name. Members may delete their content themselves before withdrawal or request deletion of personal information, and MALMOIM handles such requests in accordance with applicable law.

## Article 5 (Disclosure to Third Parties)

MALMOIM processes personal information within the purposes for which it was collected and does not disclose it to third parties except where permitted, such as on a legal basis or with valid consent.

If disclosure to a third party is necessary for event operations, external cooperation, or integration with another service, MALMOIM identifies the recipient, purpose, information, retention period, and applicable legal basis, and provides notice in accordance with applicable law.

| Service | Purpose and work performed |

## Article 7 (International Transfers of Personal Information)

MALMOIM operates its primary database using Supabase’s Seoul, Republic of Korea region (ap-northeast-2).

Website server functions are provided through Vercel, and the confirmed Functions setting is the Washington, D.C., United States region (iad1). Requests containing personal information, including login, board, and event application requests, may be processed on those servers.

Where an international transfer of personal information occurs, MALMOIM verifies a lawful basis under Article 28-8 of the Personal Information Protection Act and provides specific information about the following.

1. The legal basis for the international transfer
2. The personal information transferred
3. The destination country, timing, and method of transfer
4. The recipient’s name and contact information
5. The purposes of use and retention period
6. How and through which procedures the transfer may be refused, and the effects of refusal

**Operational verification required:** The actual contracting legal entities for Supabase and Vercel, the scope of processing in each destination country, the legal bases for outsourced processing and storage, retention periods, and refusal procedures must be verified before final publication.

## Article 8 (Destruction Procedures and Methods)

① When the retention period expires or the processing purpose has been achieved, MALMOIM destroys personal information without delay in accordance with applicable law.

② Electronic files are securely deleted in a manner that makes recovery difficult, and paper documents are destroyed by appropriate means, such as shredding or pulverization.

③ Information that must be preserved under another law is stored separately to the extent necessary and destroyed when the legally required period ends.

④ Personal information stored in backups, system logs, and external services is managed with regard to actual technical and contractual retention cycles and deletion procedures.

## Article 9 (Measures to Ensure Security)

MALMOIM implements technical and organizational protective measures required by applicable law to ensure the security of personal information.

The scope of these measures is based on those actually applied to the service, including access-right management, protection of authentication information, protection during transmission, restrictions on unauthorized access, and management of organizers’ handling of personal information.

## Article 10 (Cookies and Automatic Collection Technologies)

MALMOIM may use cookies or similar technologies to maintain login status, authenticate members, and secure the service.

Users may delete or block cookies through their browser settings. Blocking authentication cookies necessary for service operation may restrict certain functions, including login.

MALMOIM discloses the use of visitor analytics and advertising tracking tools based on the actual deployment status.

MALMOIM has decided, as an operating policy, to disable Meta Pixel advertising tracking and will update the relevant notice after confirming that tracking has stopped in the actual production deployment.

## Article 11 (Public Community Information)

Other users may view the nicknames displayed on posts and comments and the content users disclose themselves.

Private member information, such as email addresses, phone numbers, and dates of birth, is not disclosed as post author information.

Users should consider the scope of disclosure when entering personal information in content, because that information may become visible to others.

## Article 12 (Individuals’ Rights and How to Exercise Them)

Users may exercise rights under applicable law concerning their personal information, including access, correction, deletion, suspension of processing, and withdrawal of consent.

Requests concerning personal information may be submitted to the following email address.

**cometomalmoim@gmail.com**

## Article 13 (Minimum Registration Age)

Registration with MALMOIM is for people aged 19 or older.

Applicants must enter an accurate date of birth, and registration is restricted if they do not meet the eligibility requirements.

An age check based on an entered date of birth is distinct from officially recognized identity verification or personal authentication.
